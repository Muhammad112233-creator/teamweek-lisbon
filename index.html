FAQs

All flight tickets were sent to your work email address. If that is not the case, please get in touch with @marta. For anything related to the flight itself — seats, luggage, delays — contact the airline directly.

Once you land in Lisbon you have a few options: Metro line red to Alameda and then green to Rossio, the Aerobus, an Uber, or a shared taxi. It is roughly 25 minutes to the hotel.

Everybody gets a public transport pass at check-in. Keep it with you — inspectors are lovely people with zero patience.

Please take photos and videos with your phone or camera. We’d love everyone to drop their best shots in the shared Drive folder. Pre-select what you upload: keep the good moments, skip the duplicates and anything where someone doesn’t look great. If in doubt, just ask the person. And if you’d rather not be posted anywhere, tell Marta — we’ll respect that.
